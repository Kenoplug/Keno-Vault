Score Factor Breakdown
How your score is calculated
How is the Score Calculated?
Debt-to-Asset Ratio (30 pts)
Lower is better. Under 10% = 30 pts · Under 30% = 22 pts · Under 50% = 14 pts · Over 50% = 6 pts.
Liquid Cash Buffer (20 pts)
Ideal range: 10–30% = 20 pts. Below 5% = 8 pts. Over 40% = warning to deploy excess cash.
Investment Allocation (25 pts)
Over 50% invested = 25 pts · Over 30% = 18 pts · Over 15% = 10 pts · Below 15% = 5 pts.
Diversification (15 pts)
Counts positive asset types only (Cash, Physical, Investments). 3 categories = 15 pts, 2 = 10 pts, 1 = 4 pts. Liabilities excluded.
Net Worth Position (10 pts)
Graduated: over 50% margin = 10 pts · over 20% = 8 pts · over 5% = 5 pts · under 5% = 2 pts. Negative = 0 pts.
Score Grades
80–100: Excellent · 60–79: Good · 40–59: Fair · 0–39: Needs Work
