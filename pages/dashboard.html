How is the Score Calculated?
Debt-to-Asset Ratio (30 pts)
The lower your debt relative to total assets, the higher your score. Under 10% earns full marks.
Liquid Cash Buffer (20 pts)
Maintaining 10–30% in accessible cash balances security vs. opportunity cost.
Investment Ratio (25 pts)
Having 50%+ of your wealth in growth assets maximises long-term wealth building.
Diversification (15 pts)
Spreading wealth across 3–4 asset categories reduces concentration risk.
Positive Net Worth (10 pts)
Assets exceeding liabilities is the foundation of financial health.
Score Grades
80–100: Excellent · 60–79: Good · 40–59: Fair · 0–39: Needs Work
