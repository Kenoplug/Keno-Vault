FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Keno Vault.

What is Keno Vault?
Keno Vault is a personal net worth tracker that lets you log assets, track growth, simulate financial independence (FIRE), optimize debt paydown, and more — all in one premium dashboard. Built for individuals who want full visibility and control over their wealth.
Is my financial data secure?
Yes. Your data is stored on Supabase with Row-Level Security — each user can only access their own data. Pro users get AES-256 encrypted backups, a Privacy Shield (blur-sensitive values), and PIN-protected auto-lock. We do not sell or share your data.
Can I track multiple currencies?
Yes. The Multi-Currency Engine supports 160+ currencies with live exchange rates updated daily. Growth tier and above unlock full FX capabilities — pick which currencies appear as KPI cards or rate chips.
How does the FIRE Simulator work?
It calculates your Financial Independence Retire Early trajectory based on your current net worth, monthly savings, expenses, returns, and inflation. It projects your future wealth, compares it to your FI target (25× annual expenses), and tells you if and when you can retire. Pro tier unlocks this feature.
Can I share my vault with family?
Yes — the Shared Household Vault (Elite tier) lets you link up to 3 family members. You get a combined net worth dashboard while everyone keeps their individual views private. Share an invite code to get started.
